# Example — multi-shot, three shots, 15s, deferred reveal

A shipped production prompt. Read it for: shot titles that state intent, deliberate identity concealment with a timed release, and locks that protect the face from being occluded by a prop.

Annotations follow the prompt.

---

```
SCENE CONTEXT
A Japanese school corridor, late morning, early-2000s film mood. Haruka walks down the
hallway carrying a stack of textbooks piled up to just under her chin, her face visible
above them. Then, without a word, someone lifts the greater part of the stack away — we
don't yet clearly see who. She stares up, stunned, blinking twice. Only then, in the next
shot, is it revealed to be Ren: he holds the heavy books as if they were nothing, looks at
her with a calm poker face, then turns and walks on.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
<<<image_2>>> — Haruka: a female student, long straight dark hair worn down, navy blazer,
white blouse, plaid ribbon bow, grey pleated skirt, knee-high socks, loafers. Controls her
face, hair, and outfit.
<<<image_5>>> — Ren: a male student, sharp features, dark tousled hair, navy blazer with
gold buttons, white shirt, navy-and-red striped tie. Quiet and unreadable. Controls his
face, hair, and outfit — his face is kept hidden until Shot C.
<<<image_3>>> — the stack of textbooks: a stack of assorted Japanese school textbooks with
colorful spines. Controls the book stack.
<<<image_4>>> — school corridor: long upper-floor hallway, tall windows on one side over
green trees and a sports field, brown wooden sliding "2-A" doors on the other, mint-tiled
dado, glossy terrazzo floor, warm daylight. Controls the corridor.

CHARACTER NOTES
Haruka is confident but caught completely off guard — stunned, wide-eyed. Ren is closed,
still, and unreadable — he lifts the heavy books effortlessly and shows nothing; feeling
everything, showing nothing. He is taller than Haruka, but a normal, realistic student
height — clearly shorter than the corridor doors and door frames, an ordinary human
proportion, never towering or exaggeratedly tall. In Shot B his identity is deliberately
obscured; he is fully revealed only in Shot C.

LOCATION MAP
Long school corridor, late morning. Tall windows along one side over trees and a sports
field, sunlight streaming in; brown wooden sliding "2-A" doors along the other; mint-tiled
dado, glossy terrazzo floor. Haruka moving along the corridor; a figure steps in beside her.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
First frame — level, straight, well-composed: Haruka mid-corridor carrying the stack of
textbooks piled to just under her chin, her face visible above them, edging along, arms
straining. Sunlit corridor around her.

FORMAT MODE
Multi-shot sequence, 15 seconds, horizontal cinematic. Hard cuts at 5.0s and 9.5s.

SHOT A — SHE WALKS WITH THE BOOKS (0.0s to 5.0s)
OPTICS: 45° diagonal field of view, normal lens, camera at eye level, full-body
medium-wide, held level.
ACTION: Haruka walks down the corridor carrying the stack of textbooks piled to just under
her chin, her face visible above them. She takes small, careful, weight-heavy steps, arms
straining around the books, chin tucked slightly over the top of the stack to keep it
steady. The stack shifts a little; she adjusts and keeps moving. She blinks and breathes
with the effort. Sunlight falls across her from the corridor windows.

5.0s HARD CUT

SHOT B — THE BOOKS ARE LIFTED AWAY — IDENTITY HIDDEN (5.0s to 9.5s)
OPTICS: 40° diagonal field of view, normal lens. Camera positioned LOW and BEHIND the male
figure, shooting past his back and shoulder toward Haruka — so he reads only as a dark,
backlit silhouette in the foreground: the back of a navy blazer, a shoulder, an arm
reaching in. His face and features are NOT visible — he is between camera and the bright
window, rim-lit and shadowed, an anonymous male shape. Strong backlight from the corridor
window blows out behind him.
ACTION: The silhouetted arms reach in and lift the top and greater portion of the stack off
Haruka's arms in one clean, effortless motion, leaving just one book in her hands. Haruka's
face, already visible above the books, now looks UP at the figure, startled, wide-eyed,
caught completely off guard. She blinks — blink, blink — two clear, distinct blinks, each
with a soft audible blink sound. Her lips part slightly in surprise. We still do not see
who took the books — only his dark back and arms.

9.5s HARD CUT

SHOT C — REVEAL: HE HOLDS THEM, THEN WALKS ON (9.5s to 15.0s)
OPTICS: 40° diagonal field of view, normal lens, camera at eye level on the two of them
from the side / three-quarter front, now clearly showing Ren's face for the first time.
Haruka in frame looking up at him (he is moderately taller), sunlight from the window
across him.
ACTION: Now revealed as Ren, he stands holding the big stack of books easily in his arms,
as if their weight were nothing, completely relaxed. Sunlight from the window falls across
him. He looks down at Haruka with a calm, unreadable poker face — no smile, no words, a
level gaze — holds it a beat, then turns away and walks off down the corridor with the
books, unhurried. Haruka stays where she is, the single book in her hands.

PHYSICS
The stack has real, solid weight — it presses into Haruka's arms and shifts with her steps
in Shot A. When the figure lifts it, the mass transfers cleanly; the one remaining book is
light in her hands. Ren carries the heavy stack with steady, effortless control, no strain.
Haruka's hair and shoulders shift as the weight leaves her. Backlight flares behind the
silhouette in Shot B; sunlight shifts across Ren as he turns in Shot C. His walk is
settled, heel to toe. Curtains stir faintly at the windows.

LIGHTING
Bright milky late-morning daylight through the tall corridor windows. In Shot B, strong
backlight from the window turns the male figure into a dark, rim-lit silhouette with his
face in shadow — identity obscured. In Shot C, warm sunlight from the window falls across
Ren's face and shoulders as he's revealed. Calm, ordinary daylight, warm on the terrazzo.

STYLE
Early-2000s Japanese film look — soft natural light, milky low-contrast color, gentle
desaturation, fine 35mm grain, quiet stillness. Iwai Shunji / early Kore-eda texture.
Naturalistic, unhurried, intimate. Steady, level, well-composed framing.

AUDIO
Diegetic sound only. No music. Shot A — careful footsteps on terrazzo, the creak of the
shifting book stack, Haruka's strained breaths. Shot B — the soft slide of the books
lifting away, then two clear soft blink sounds — blink, blink — and a tiny caught breath.
Shot C — quiet corridor tone, Ren's unhurried footsteps as he walks off, faint wind at the
windows. No dialogue — no one speaks.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Every shot is level, straight, and well-composed. In every shot the stack of books reaches
only up to Haruka's CHIN and no higher — her FACE stays fully visible above the books at
all times. Shot A: Haruka carries the chin-high stack with visible effort. Shot B: the
person who lifts the books is deliberately NOT identifiable — shot from low and behind him
as a dark, backlit silhouette (back of navy blazer, shoulder, reaching arm), his face NOT
visible; the books lift away leaving ONE book in Haruka's hands; her face looks up,
startled, and she blinks twice — blink, blink — with a soft audible blink sound each time.
Shot C is the REVEAL — Ren's face clearly shown for the first time, moderately taller than
her, sunlight on him, holding the big stack effortlessly with a calm unreadable poker face,
then turning and walking off down the corridor. Ren is only moderately taller than Haruka —
a natural, realistic student height, clearly SHORTER than the corridor doors and door
frames; his head never reaches the top of the doorways, and he is never towering or giant.
Ren says nothing and shows no expression; Haruka does not speak. Both faces blink and
breathe naturally — never frozen or masklike. Corridor matches <<<image_4>>>; books match
<<<image_3>>>; identities, hair, and outfits match <<<image_2>>> and <<<image_5>>>. No
dialogue is spoken. Natural smooth movements. High detail. 4K Ultra HD. Sharp clarity.
Stable lighting. Consistent frame rate. Clean picture.
```

## What to learn from it

**The reveal is engineered across four blocks.** ACTIVE REFERENCES carries `his face is kept hidden until Shot C`. CHARACTER NOTES repeats it. OPTICS in Shot B specifies the camera position that achieves it — LOW and BEHIND, shooting past his back into the window. LIGHTING supplies the backlight that completes it, then explicitly restores front light in Shot C. POSITIVE LOCKS restates both states. Concealment is a mechanism, not a wish.

**Backlight is used deliberately here.** Compare with the general rule that backlighting a reference-locked face defeats the lock. That is exactly why it is used — and why Shot C reverses it in the same sentence.

**No dialogue anywhere.** The concealed figure and the unreadable character both get silence. The emotional content is carried by two audible blinks. This is the correct handling of an anonymous body.

**The chin lock protects identity.** `the stack of books reaches only up to Haruka's CHIN and no higher — her FACE stays fully visible above the books at all times.` A prop tall enough to occlude the face would disable her reference. Stated in SCENE CONTEXT, FIRST FRAME, ACTION, and LOCKS.

**Height is constrained numerically-in-words.** `clearly SHORTER than the corridor doors and door frames; his head never reaches the top of the doorways` — an in-frame comparison, not an abstract measurement.

**Gate 9 handled in one clause.** `Curtains stir faintly at the windows.`

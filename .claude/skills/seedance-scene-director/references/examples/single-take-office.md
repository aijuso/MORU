# Example — single continuous take, 12s, VFX character in a live-action room

A shipped production prompt in the single-take idiom. Read it for: top-level CAMERA/OPTICS/ACTION TIMING, semantic aliasing, frame coordinates, absence with a plan, and an action that must not complete.

---

```
SCENE CONTEXT
A young man alone in a warm sunlit home office stares at his laptop in tense thought,
hardens into anger, pulls a baseball bat from under the desk and coils into a windup to
smash the screen. At the top of the windup a 3 cm fairy shoots up from behind the laptop
lid in a burst of screen light and the man flinches hard in fright. The fairy, facing him
head-on, delivers his line while flitting lightly back and forth, then loops gracefully
back down behind the lid into the screen as the man's eyes follow him all the way.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@LOC_OFFICE (image 1): warm home office with herringbone parquet, a large three-pane
picture window overlooking treetops and low city buildings, a light desk centered in front
of the window, black swivel chair on casters, metal shelving unit and potted plant on the
left, white console with mushroom lamps and a large portrait painting on the right, brown
leather sofa at the right edge. Reference controls room geometry, furniture placement,
materials and warm daylight atmosphere only. The reference image's camera angle and framing
are not inherited; this shot uses its own camera defined below.
@MAN (image 2): mid-20s lean man, shoulder-length light-brown hair, light stubble, pale
yellow chore jacket over white t-shirt, cream trousers; current state: brooding focus
turning to anger, then a hard startled fright, then wary amazement. 100% matches the
reference.
@FAIRY (image 3): elderly gentleman fairy exactly 3 cm tall — grey curly hair, gold
rectangular glasses, grey mustache and small chin tuft, gold hoop earrings, pointed elf
ears, brown herringbone tweed three-piece suit with bow tie, white shirt, pocket square,
brown shoes, slender wooden wand in his right hand. He has no wings and flies by smooth
wingless levitation. He does not glow or emit any light: a matte, unlit miniature figure
lit only by the room's daylight, keeping his stylized 3D-toon look from the reference.
Voice: spry, warm, confident elderly male voice, light and quick. 100% matches the
reference.
@BAT (image 4): wooden baseball bat, amber handle fading into a dark green barrel. 100%
matches the reference.

LOCATION MAP
The desk stands centered in front of the large picture window; @MAN sits at it in the black
swivel chair facing the window and his laptop, his back to the room. The camera works from
behind the laptop, between the desk and the window, looking at @MAN — the laptop's
closed-side lid faces camera in the foreground, its display faces him. Also on the desk: an
articulated architect lamp, a small ceramic cup, a few books, a small plant, a stack of
books at one end. Behind @MAN in the depth of the room: the shelving unit and tall potted
plant on one side, the white console, portrait painting and leather sofa on the other. @BAT
lies under the desk, leaning against the near desk leg, handle toward @MAN. Warm daylight
pours through the window behind camera onto his face and the desk, laying long soft sun
patches across the parquet.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
The video opens from behind the laptop. The first visible frame already shows: the laptop
lid's back filling the lower foreground, x 25–70%, y 65–95%; @MAN directly behind it at
center, seated, framed head-and-chest above the lid, x 40–62%, y 20–65%, face toward
camera, eyes down on the screen just below the lens — never into the lens; the desk surface
with cup and books flanking the lid; the room falling away behind him in soft focus. @BAT
is partly visible under the desk at the frame's lower edge, leaning against the desk leg.
No empty establishing frame. @FAIRY is intentionally absent until 5.5s: he is inside the
laptop, shoots up from behind the lid at 5.5s, and dives back down behind it at 10.2s.
Exactly two characters appear in this shot: @MAN and @FAIRY; the room is otherwise empty.

FORMAT MODE
SINGLE CONTINUOUS TAKE. Real-time motion. No cuts, no montage, no transition effects.

OPTICS
47° diagonal field of view, standard normal lens character, natural human-eye perspective,
zero distortion. Camera sits about 1 meter behind the laptop and 1.5 meters from @MAN's
face, holding that distance for the whole take — close enough that his expressions and the
3 cm fairy read large and sharp. Shallow comfortable depth of field: the laptop lid edge,
the man and the fairy sharp; the room behind him melts into soft warm bokeh. LENS LOCK 47°,
no drift, no zoom, no push-in.

CAMERA
Handheld camera positioned behind the laptop, slightly above desk height, framing @MAN
frontally over the lid. It stays in this one position for the whole take, alive with light
handheld shake — natural breath sway, micro-settling, tiny weight shifts, small human
corrections — never digital jitter. At 3.0s the operator tilts down briefly as @MAN leans
under the desk, then tilts up from the same spot to keep his head and the raised bat in
frame as he rises into the windup, the laptop lid staying anchored at the bottom of frame.
At 5.5s the camera gives one tiny startled jolt as the fairy shoots up from behind the lid,
then breathes quietly in place through the dialogue, easing a few degrees down at 10.2s as
the fairy dives back behind the lid. No arcs, no tracking, no reframing beyond these small
in-place moves.

ACTION TIMING
0.0s–2.0s: frontal over the lid. @MAN stares at the screen in brooding thought — still
hands, slow blink, thumb pressed against his lips, eyes down on the display.
2.0s–3.0s: the thought curdles into anger: jaw tightens, nostrils flare, eyes narrow, one
hard exhale; his hand slides off his lips into a fist.
3.0s–4.2s: he leans down out of frame center, pulls @BAT out from under the desk by the
handle with his right hand, wraps both hands around it and springs up out of the chair; the
swivel chair rolls back half a meter on the parquet and settles; the camera tilts up with
him.
4.2s–5.5s: standing over the desk, he coils into a full windup — bat raised high past his
shoulder, knuckles whitening, torso loaded, eyes locked down on the screen, one sharp
inhale. The swing itself never begins; the bat never descends.
5.5s–6.2s: at the very top of the windup a white-green light flares up from behind the
laptop lid, washing over his face and the desk, and @FAIRY shoots straight up over the
lid's top edge in a frantic hurry — a fast tumbling launch — then rights himself in mid-air
by smooth wingless levitation, squarely face-to-face with @MAN at the center of frame,
whole body and gaze aimed directly at the man's eyes, both arms thrust straight forward,
palms out, wand still in his right fist, his tweed back and grey curls to camera. The flare
behind the lid dies immediately; the fairy himself emits no light. @MAN startles hard: his
whole body jerks, he stumbles half a step back, shoulders jumping, the raised bat wavering,
eyes flying wide, a sharp audible gasp — real fright, bigger than surprise, in full frontal
view.
6.2s–8.8s: @FAIRY holds the air between the lid's top edge and @MAN's face, about 40 cm
from his eyes, around x 50%, y 42%. He never freezes in place: he glides lightly back and
forth — small darting passes a few centimeters left, right, up and down, like a man pacing
in mid-air — always keeping his face and torso turned squarely toward @MAN. At 6.5s he
says: "Hold up, let me show you how to do this right." He lowers his arms mid-line and
plants his free hand on his hip while still drifting in his small flight pattern. @MAN
stays frozen, bat half-raised, chest heaving, wide frightened eyes tracking the little
movements just over the lid.
8.8s–10.4s: the fairy finishes, gives a tiny salute with the wand, banks into a graceful
arc — one smooth curving loop over the desk, staying in frame — and dives back down behind
the laptop lid; the white-green flare pulses up from behind the lid once and fades, its
reflection dying on @MAN's face.
10.4s–12.0s: @MAN's eyes track the whole dive down to the hidden screen and stay there; he
stands still, bat drifting down to his side, lips parted, fright melting into stunned
wonder. Hold on his frontal stunned face over the quiet lid.

PHYSICS
The bat has real wooden mass: dragging it from under the desk scrapes lightly on the
parquet, lifting it loads his shoulders, the windup coils his torso, and the startled
stumble shows real weight shift — half a step back, heel catching, balance recovered. The
swivel chair rolls with real caster friction on the wood floor. @FAIRY flies without wings:
a smooth, controlled levitation with believable momentum — he banks into turns, decelerates
before changing direction, bobs gently as he moves, trouser legs and jacket hem swaying
slightly with each pass; the exit is one clean banked arc down behind the lid. He receives
the room's daylight like any physical object. His 3 cm scale stays constant relative to the
laptop lid and the man's face. The cup, lamp and books never move. The jacket creases and
swings naturally with the reach, the windup and the flinch.

LIGHTING
Warm intimate interior daylight. Primary light: soft golden daylight through the big
picture window behind camera, falling frontally onto @MAN's face, the laptop lid and the
desk; long warm sun patches stretch across the parquet and the room behind him glows in
soft warm bokeh. His face is fully lit and readable with soft shadow rolloff and clear eye
catchlights, so the brooding, the fright and the final wonder all read in full frontal
view. Exposure priority: his face. The fairy emits no light and casts no glow; the only
emitted light is the screen's brief white-green flare from behind the lid at 5.5s and again
at 10.2s — seen as a wash on his face and a glow over the lid's edge, each fading within
half a second. Warm filmic color, gentle grain, soft naturalistic ad-photo texture matching
the location reference.

AUDIO
Quiet room tone, birdsong and faint street sounds behind glass; a slow thoughtful breath,
then a hard angry exhale. The bat scrapes softly on the parquet as he pulls it from under
the desk, the chair casters roll, his grip creaks at the top of the windup — then a bright
frantic zip-whoosh at 5.5s, answered by @MAN's sharp frightened gasp. At 6.5s @FAIRY speaks
the only line, close and clean over ducked room tone, in his spry elderly voice: "Hold up,
let me show you how to do this right." At 8.8s a soft airy whoosh follows his looping arc,
ending in a small electronic blip as he drops behind the lid and the flare fades. @MAN says
no words; beyond the single gasp his lips stay still. No music, no subtitles.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly two characters: @MAN and @FAIRY. The strike never happens: the bat stays raised at
the top of the windup, never descends, and the laptop remains intact and untouched for the
entire shot; there is no desk hit anywhere. The camera stays behind the laptop the whole
take: the lid anchors the bottom of frame, @MAN stays frontal, and his eyes stay on the
screen or the fairy — never into the lens. @FAIRY has no wings at any point and flies by
smooth levitation only; he stays exactly 3 cm tall, emits no light or glow, keeps his face
and torso turned squarely toward @MAN whenever he is out of the screen, and never holds a
static hover — he glides lightly back and forth in small passes while speaking. @MAN's
fright at 5.5s is strong and full-bodied, and his eyes visibly follow the fairy's exit dive
all the way down behind the lid. All identities, costumes and the bat's green-to-amber
finish 100% match their references. The fairy keeps his stylized 3D-toon render style
inside the live-action scene. One continuous handheld take from one position: breathing
shake, one tilt down and up during the reach and rise, one tiny jolt at 5.5s, one small
tilt down on the exit, nothing more.
```

## What to learn from it

**Aliases, bound once.** `@MAN (image 2)` binds the slot; the body never uses a raw token again. In a prompt this long with this many repetitions, a number swap would be invisible. An alias swap would not.

**No STYLE block.** The look is carried in LIGHTING's final sentence: `Warm filmic color, gentle grain, soft naturalistic ad-photo texture matching the location reference.` Valid — do not add a STYLE block just because the template has one.

**Coordinates where composition is load-bearing.** The laptop lid must stay anchored at the bottom of frame for the whole take, and a 3 cm character must stay findable. `x 25–70%, y 65–95%` and `around x 50%, y 42%` do that. A conventional medium shot would not need them.

**Absence stated with a mechanism and two timestamps.** `@FAIRY is intentionally absent until 5.5s: he is inside the laptop, shoots up from behind the lid at 5.5s, and dives back down behind it at 10.2s.`

**The windup that must not complete**, denied in three places: ACTION TIMING (`The swing itself never begins; the bat never descends`), POSITIVE LOCKS (`the bat stays raised at the top of the windup, never descends, and the laptop remains intact and untouched`), and SCENE CONTEXT by omission of any strike. Note the phrasing: a **maintained state** first, prohibition second.

**Emission control at four levels** — the reference line, ACTION TIMING, LIGHTING, and LOCKS all say the fairy does not glow. Models make magical things glow; one denial is not enough.

**An exhaustive camera whitelist closed with `nothing more`.** Every permitted move has a timestamp and a motivation. Anything unlisted is forbidden, and the closing phrase makes that explicit rather than implied.

**One speaker, face visible, line placed at a stated time.** Gate 1 and Gate 3 both pass trivially — which is why this prompt worked.

# Prompt lexicon

Ready-to-use, precise vocabulary. Pull from these so the prompt reads specific instead of generic. Don't dump whole lists into a prompt — pick the few terms that fit the intended look.

## Shot types
extreme wide / establishing shot · wide shot · full shot · medium wide · medium · medium close-up · close-up · extreme close-up · two-shot · over-the-shoulder · insert / detail shot · aerial / drone shot · top-down flat-lay.

## Angles & framing
eye-level · slightly elevated (from the stands) · high angle · low angle · worm's-eye · bird's-eye / top-down · three-quarter (3/4) view · profile · frontal / orthographic · Dutch tilt (use sparingly) · centered symmetric · rule-of-thirds off-center · leading lines into frame · deep foreground framing.

## Times of day & light quality
pre-dawn blue hour · early morning (8–9 AM), soft hazy · mid-morning, clear · harsh midday, short hard shadows · overcast, flat soft light · golden hour, warm low sun and long shadows · sunset · dusk / twilight · blue hour · night, artificial sources · moonlit.
Qualities: soft · diffused · hazy · hard · directional · dappled · rim / backlit · ambient · luminous near the horizon.

## Lighting setups
soft key from camera-left · window light, soft and directional · overcast softbox-like ambient · three-point studio · high-key (bright, low shadow) · low-key (dark, dramatic) · rim / backlight separating subject from background · golden-hour backlight with lens flare · even orthographic model-sheet lighting (no dramatic shadows) · practical sources (neon, lamps, screens) at night.

## Camera bodies & looks
large-format cinema camera (ARRI Alexa look) · anamorphic look (oval bokeh, horizontal flares) · spherical prime (clean, no distortion) · medium-format stills look (Hasselblad, ultra detail) · 35mm film look · 16mm grainy documentary look · vintage rangefinder look · smartphone-casual look (when realism/snapshot is wanted).

## Lens character
clean spherical prime · fast prime, shallow depth of field · wide-angle (24–35mm) establishing · portrait focal length (85mm) flattering compression · macro (100mm) for objects · telephoto compression, flattened planes · mild vignetting · subtle natural lens character · deep focus (everything sharp) · focus falls off quickly behind subject · focus-stacked (product).

## Color-grade vocabulary
naturalistic / true-to-life grade · warm-neutral white balance · restrained / muted saturation · gentle low contrast · soft lifted blacks · subtle film-like halation in bright areas · fine photochemical grain · teal-and-orange (use consciously, it reads commercial) · desaturated / bleach-bypass · high-contrast punchy · pastel / soft · "documentary-realistic, not a glossy ad look."

## Film stock references
Kodak Portra (warm, soft skin) · Kodak Gold (nostalgic warm) · Fuji Pro 400H (cool-neutral, gentle) · Cinestill 800T (tungsten, halated night) · Ektachrome (crisp, saturated slides) · Tri-X (classic B&W grain).

## Atmosphere & scale cues
morning haze softening the far bank · thin wispy high clouds · light mist over grass · dust motes in a light shaft · a lone distant figure for scale · freshly-mowed mowing patterns · faint painted markings · weathered / lived-in surfaces · condensation, reflections in wet ground · steam, smoke, or breath in cold air.

## Negatives library
Universal: no on-screen text · no watermark · no visible brand logos or advertising · no signature.
People: no crowd · no extra people · (or, for anatomy) no extra limbs · no distorted anatomy · no fused fingers.
Character sheets: no mismatched features between panels · no inconsistent proportions · no busy background.
Quality: no blur (unless intended) · no lowres · no jpeg artifacts · no oversaturation · no harsh HDR / glossy ad look (when going for realism).
Composition: no cluttered background · no warped perspective · no crooked / tilted horizon (unless intended).

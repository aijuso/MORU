# Layer templates by subject

Each template lists the labeled layers to build, in order. Fill every layer with concrete, nameable detail (see the craft rules in SKILL.md). The opening line, the LIGHT / COLOR GRADE / CAMERA & LENS trio, and the technical tail are shared across all templates.

---

## Scene / location / establishing shot

Front-to-back spatial layers. This is the richest template.

1. **Opening line** — shot type + place + time of day + mood/emptiness.
2. **FOREGROUND** — what's nearest camera and frames the shot (a roof edge, railings, near seating, foliage, a doorway). Include what it does to the light (casts the near area in shadow, catches warm rim light).
3. **MIDGROUND / the hero surface** — the main subject of the space (the track, the plaza, the room's furniture), with its materials, markings, wear, and one or two scale/life cues.
4. **BACKDROP** — everything beyond: skyline, hills, far wall, water, treeline, softened by haze/distance.
5. **LIGHT & SKY** → **COLOR GRADE** → **CAMERA & LENS** → **tail**.

---

## Character sheet / turnaround

Panel/view layers. Emphasis on consistency across views.

1. **Opening line** — "Character sheet of [who], [key trait]. [N]-panel reference layout on [background]."
2. **PANEL 1 — close-up** — head-and-shoulders, framing, expression described concretely (bright alert eyes, slight asymmetric smile), skin/hair texture, lighting that reveals structure.
3. **PANEL 2 — full body** — front and back (or 3/4) views, pose (neutral A-pose, arms slightly out), and an explicit **consistency lock**: same proportions, wardrobe, hairstyle, palette across every view.
4. **WARDROBE / DESIGN** — garments, materials, colors, distinctive props or accessories, named.
5. **STYLE** — concept-art / model-sheet / anime / 3D render; even orthographic-style lighting; flat neutral background; no dramatic shadows.
6. **Tail** — consistent character design, sharp focus; negatives: mismatched features between panels, extra limbs, distorted anatomy, text, watermark, busy background.

Character sheets usually skip the heavy COLOR GRADE block (flat even light is the point) but keep an explicit STYLE line.

---

## Portrait (single framing)

1. **Opening line** — shot type (close-up / medium) + who + mood + setting in one breath.
2. **SUBJECT & FACE** — age/features/expression/gaze, skin and hair texture, what the face is doing.
3. **WARDROBE** — garments and materials, named.
4. **ENVIRONMENT** — the setting behind them and how far out of focus it falls.
5. **LIGHT** (key direction & quality, e.g. soft window light from camera-left) → **COLOR GRADE** → **CAMERA & LENS** (portrait focal length, shallow depth of field, where focus falls) → **tail**.

---

## Object / prop / product

Coarse-to-fine.

1. **Opening line** — "[shot type] of [the hero object], [material/character], on [surface/backdrop]."
2. **THE OBJECT** — form, silhouette, proportions, condition (pristine / worn / weathered).
3. **MATERIAL & DETAIL** — surface, texture, finish, edges, wear marks, small telltale details.
4. **SURFACE & BACKDROP** — what it sits on and against; keep it clean unless a styled scene is wanted.
5. **LIGHT** (studio setup or natural, and what the highlights/reflections do) → **COLOR GRADE** (or simplified) → **CAMERA & LENS** (macro / product focal length, focus stacking, angle) → **tail** (aspect ratio, seamless/backdrop note, no text/logos).

## Worked example — character sheet

**Brief:** "character sheet of a young man with an expressive face — two panels, close-up and full body front and back, grey background."

**Delivered shape:**
- Opening: *Character sheet of a young man, expressive face. Two-panel reference layout on a flat neutral grey background.*
- PANEL 1: close-up, front-facing, bright alert eyes, raised brows, hint of a smile, tousled hair, even soft studio light revealing structure.
- PANEL 2: full body, front view left / back view right, neutral A-pose, feet shoulder-width — **consistency lock:** identical proportions, wardrobe, hairstyle across both.
- WARDROBE: named garments (e.g. casual hoodie and slim jeans, canvas sneakers).
- STYLE: clean concept-art model-sheet, orthographic even lighting, flat grey backdrop, no props.
- Tail: consistent design, sharp focus; avoid mismatched features between panels, extra limbs, distorted anatomy, text, watermark.

## Worked example — object

**Brief:** "prompt for a vintage brass compass."

**Delivered shape:** opening fixes it as a close product shot on a dark slate surface; THE OBJECT (worn brass pocket compass, hinged lid open); MATERIAL & DETAIL (patinated brass, fine engraved bearings, scratched glass, a smudge of tarnish); SURFACE & BACKDROP (matte dark slate, soft seamless falloff); LIGHT (soft key from upper-left, gentle specular highlight tracing the rim); CAMERA & LENS (100mm macro look, focus stacked, slight top-down angle); tail (1:1, ultra detail, no text, no logos, clean backdrop).
